About RoleRadar

RoleRadar brings job search, resume feedback, and interview practice into one place so you are not juggling five different tools. Search real listings from top job platforms, get AI feedback on your resume against a specific posting or role, and rehearse with AI-generated interview questions before you walk into the real one. Your saved jobs and applications stay with your account, so you always know where you stand.
